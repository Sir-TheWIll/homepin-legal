HomePin and HomePin Pro

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Account deletion

Delete your account

You can delete your HomePin (customer) or HomePin Pro account and request deletion of associated personal data. This page is the web resource for that request. You do not need the app installed to use the email option below.

In the app

Open HomePin or HomePin Pro → You → Account → Delete account. Confirm when asked. Deletion is blocked while you have jobs in progress; finish or cancel those jobs first, then try again.

Request by email (web)

Email support@homepin.app with the subject “Account deletion request”. Include:

1. The name on the account
2. The email you use to sign in
3. Whether the account is HomePin (customer) or HomePin Pro

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm you control the email address on the account.

What we delete and what we keep

After deletion we scrub personal identifiers such as email and sign-in id from your profile. Completed job and chat history that the other party still needs may remain with limited identity information, as described in the Privacy Policy. Lead-credit purchases and records we must keep for billing, safety, or law may also be retained where required. Unused lead credits have no cash value except where required by law.

Suspended or limited accounts

We may limit matching, remove content, or suspend an account that violates the Terms of Service or applicable law (for example harassment, fraud, or scam). If you believe your HomePin or HomePin Pro account was suspended or limited in error, email support@homepin.app with the subject “Account suspension appeal”. Include the email on the account, which app you use, and a short description of what happened.

This page is for HomePin and HomePin Pro users. Developer Play Console appeals go through Google, not this form.
