Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how HomePin ("we") collects, uses, and shares information in the HomePin customer app and HomePin Pro. It applies when you create an account, post or take jobs, chat, or contact support. Contact: support@homepin.app.

2. Information we collect

Account: name, email, password or Google sign-in identifiers, role (customer or pro), and optional phone and profile photo. Customer: property addresses, project details, photos you attach, quotes you receive, jobs, and reviews. Pro: business profile, services, service area (including map location and radius), hours, lead preferences, after-job photos, quotes, lead and credit history, and earnings records for in-person jobs. Communications: in-app messages (including offline drafts stored on your device) and live support chats. Device: push tokens, app version, and approximate diagnostics needed to run the apps. Location: used when you allow it for local matching or the pro job map.

3. How we use it

We use this information to operate the marketplace: authenticate you, match customers and pros, deliver chat and notifications, process pro lead-credit purchases, verify job completion with after photos, enforce blocks and reports, prevent fraud and spam, and provide customer support. We may also send optional promotional notifications if you enable them.

4. Who we share with

Matched customers and pros see the profile, project, quote, job, and chat information needed to do the work. We use processors that help us run HomePin, including Supabase (authentication), Convex (app data and files), Stripe (pro credit purchases only), tawk.to (live support chat), Mapbox and Google Maps (addresses and routing), and Expo/device push services. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the platform.

5. Support chat (tawk.to)

If you use Live chat in Help, the conversation runs on tawk.to. We may send your display name, email, HomePin user id, and whether you are a customer or pro so support can identify you. tawk.to's own privacy policy applies to data it processes as our live-chat provider.

6. Payments

Customers do not pay HomePin for jobs. Job payment is in person with the pro. Pros buy lead credits with Stripe; card details are handled by Stripe, not stored in full on HomePin servers.

7. Retention

We keep account and job records while your account is active and as needed for the other party's history, billing of lead credits, safety, and legal obligations. After you delete your account we scrub identifiers such as email and sign-in id from your profile. Completed job and chat history that the other party still needs may remain with limited identity information.

8. Your choices

You can edit your profile, notification preferences, and (for pros) services and area in the app. You can request a copy of your data from Legal and privacy; we aim to respond within 30 days. You can delete your account after finishing or canceling open jobs. You can disable location or notifications in system settings, though some features will not work without them.

9. Children

HomePin is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted transport and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@homepin.app.
